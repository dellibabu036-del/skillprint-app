⚡ About Skillprint

Learn • Quiz • Career • Chat — All in one super app for Indian students

🎯 Our Mission

Skillprint was built with one goal: give every Indian student free access to world-class learning, exam prep, and career guidance — regardless of their college, city, or income.

From Python to UPSC, from resume building to AI-powered career paths — everything a student needs, in one place, forever free (with an optional Pro tier for power users).
