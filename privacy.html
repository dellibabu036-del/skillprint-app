🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Skillprint ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Local data (stored only on your device): XP, badges, course progress, quiz scores, chat history, uploads. This never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.
  • Account data (if you sign up): Display name, email address. Stored locally in your browser unless Firebase is configured.
  • Analytics (if enabled): Anonymous usage statistics (pages visited, buttons clicked) to improve the app.
  • Advertising (if enabled): Ad networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads. See Section 5.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the Skillprint experience
  • To save your progress across sessions
  • To show personalised content (courses, quizzes, career paths)
  • To respond to support requests
  • To serve ads (for free-tier users)

3. Data Storage

Skillprint is a client-side web application. Most data is stored using browser LocalStorage and IndexedDB. This means:

  • Data stays on your device
  • Clearing browser data will erase your progress
  • Data does not sync between devices unless you export/import manually

4. Third-Party Services

Skillprint may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (optional chat feature) — for real-time messaging
  • Google AdSense (if enabled) — for advertising
  • Pollinations.ai — for AI image generation
  • Google Analytics (if enabled) — for anonymous usage analytics

Each service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

5. Advertising & Cookies

Free-tier users may see advertisements served by Google AdSense or other ad networks. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Skillprint is intended for users aged 13 and above. If you are under 13, please do not use the app without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (via the Export feature in Settings)
  • Delete your data (via the Reset feature in Settings)
  • Opt out of analytics or ads (via Settings or browser controls)
  • Request data deletion by contacting us

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@skillprint.app.
